🏪1. About NearStock

NearStock is a hyperlocal discovery platform that helps customers find products and services available at shops near their location across India. The platform also enables shop owners ("Shopkeepers") to list and manage their products and services.

NearStock is operated as an independent platform. Our registered contact is support@nearstock.in and our website is nearstock.in.

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the NearStock mobile application available on Google Play Store, our website at nearstock.in, and any associated services (including features such as our website feedback form).

⚙️4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display nearby shops, products and services based on your location
  • To enable shopkeepers to list and manage their inventory
  • To facilitate voluntary inquiries between customers and shopkeepers
  • To provide in-app notifications relevant to your account
  • To show shopkeepers anonymous statistics about their shop engagement
  • To improve the App's features, performance, and user experience
  • To detect, investigate and prevent fraudulent or illegal activity
  • To communicate important updates, maintenance notices, or policy changes
  • To respond to your support requests and complaints
We process your data only for legitimate purposes directly related to providing the NearStock service. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

🤝5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.

5.1 When We Share Information

  • With Shopkeepers (only on your consent): Your name and phone number are shared with a shopkeeper ONLY when you voluntarily submit an Inquiry. See Section 6.
  • With Service Providers: We use trusted third-party service providers to operate the App (cloud storage, authentication, analytics). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes. See Section 8.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required by Indian law, regulation, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of the business, your data may be transferred. You will be notified via the App or email before any such transfer occurs.
  • Protection of Rights: To enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of NearStock, our users, or the public.

5.2 What Is Never Shared

  • Your precise real-time location with any third party
  • Your personal data with advertisers
  • Your browsing activity within the App to other users
  • Your contact details unless you explicitly consent via Inquiry
🛡️ Customer browsing is completely anonymous. Shopkeepers only receive aggregate view counts — no individual identity is shared unless you initiate an Inquiry.

📩6. Inquiry & Consent System

NearStock operates a voluntary, consent-based inquiry system:

  • By default, all customer browsing is completely anonymous
  • If you wish to contact a shopkeeper, you may tap the "Send Inquiry" button on any product or service page
  • Before submitting, a clear consent dialog will appear stating exactly what information will be shared and with whom
  • You must actively tap "Share & Send" to proceed — no data is shared automatically
  • Once submitted, the shopkeeper receives your name, phone number, and your message
  • You may contact us at support@nearstock.in to request deletion of any submitted inquiry
Your contact information is NEVER shared with any shopkeeper without your explicit, informed consent. This system complies with the consent requirements under India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable data protection principles.

📍7. Location Data

  • The App requests access to your device's location to show you nearby shops and calculate distances
  • Your precise location coordinates are processed on-device and used only to calculate proximity to shops. They are not stored on our servers
  • You may deny location permission at any time through your device settings. The App will remain functional but distance-based features will be unavailable
  • We do not track your location in the background when the App is closed
  • Location data is never shared with third parties for advertising purposes
  • We use the Android Location API in accordance with Google Play policies

🔐9. Data Security

Important: No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

If you suspect any security vulnerability in the App, please report it immediately to support@nearstock.in. We request that you do not publicly disclose any security issue until we have had reasonable time to address it.
